# Keyboard / Emoji Cheat-Sheet Overlays (⌥, ⌥⇧, ⌘⌃)

- **⌨️ Hold-to-see cheat-sheets (`KeymapOverlay`)** — holding a modifier long enough to be *asking* rather than *typing* draws a keyboard on the secondary screen. Four sheets, one renderer, one hold delay (`KeymapHoldCoordinator.delay`: 0.3 s multi-monitor, 1.0 s single, since on one screen the sheet covers what you're doing): **⌥ / ⌥⇧ / ⌃⌥** show the emoji/character each key emits, read from `EmojiKeyLayer` — the same map the event tap types from, not the `.keylayout`, which stopped being the source of those characters when the app took the rewrite over; **⌘⌃** shows what this app's own ⌘⌃ shortcuts *do*, one word per key (`terminal`, `claude` on C (**Q says `salut` with a 👋 accent**: it said `claude` too while ⌘⌃Q opened a permissions-bypassed terminal, and two identical words on one board is a key you press wrong at speed. It left the sheet on 2026-09-09, when the 🤖 mascot moved to ⌃⌥G and the combination went back to macOS's Lock Screen, and came back with it on 2026-09-10 — ⌃⌥ became the third emoji board and G was needed for the goose, so the wave returned to the key it was written for and shadows Lock Screen again), `notes`, `wispr🎙️`, `tile`, `SRL`, `zoom`, `email`, `catalog`, `todo`, `relay` on D with a 🎯 accent). Three keys answer with a **picture instead of a word**, because a mark is recognised faster than the name of the thing: **L = 📅** (calendar), **G = the Gmail logo** and **N = the Google Docs logo** (the "notes" doc itself — the mark *is* the destination, where S's `notes` is the act of sending something there). The two emoji stay in `CommandControlShortcuts.labels` and are detected by the pure `KeymapOverlayRenderer.isPictogram` — a label that is *nothing but* emoji is drawn big and centred in the whole payload area instead of being shrunk to word size at the bottom (`wispr🎙️` is a word that merely *contains* one, so it must not qualify). The Gmail and Docs marks are bundled PNGs (`Resources/gmail-logo.png`, `Resources/gdocs-logo.png`, listed in `CommandControlShortcuts.artworkNames`, loaded through `artworkImages()`) drawn **straight onto the key with its own transparency intact — no white plate behind it**: every key on this sheet is one flat black, so a white card under a single one is the loudest thing on the whole keyboard. The mark survives the dark background because the M *is* the coloured part; the envelope body Gmail normally paints white simply reads as key. A key carrying artwork counts as **bound** even though its `labels` entry is absent, so `boundKeyCodes` (labels ∪ artwork) — not `labels.keys` — is what the sync test asserts. Four keys also carry a small **corner accent** (`CommandControlShortcuts.accents`) in the top-right, opposite the base letter: **E = @**, **Z = 🔗**, **S = 🚀**, **R = 📋**, **K = 📕** and **M = ✉️**. Their words ("email", "zoom", "notes", "SRL", "catalog", "todo") are nouns — the *thing*, not what happens to it — so the mark supplies the missing verb a beat faster: 🚀 says the selection is launched into the notes, 📋 that the SRL details are pasted rather than opened, 📕 which book "catalog" means; it is drawn at 28pt against the letter's 42 precisely so it reads as a hint on the key and not as a second key label. A test asserts every accented key is one that's actually bound — an accent on a dimmed key would decorate nothing. Which sheet a combination asks for is the pure, unit-tested `KeymapHoldCoordinator.sheet` — deliberately exact, so anything with ⌘ on top of ⌥ (⌘⌃⌥D dark mode, ⌘⌥ generally) shows nothing rather than guessing. **⌃⌥ was in that list until 2026-09-10** and now has a sheet of its own; it was still a shortcut prefix too (⌃⌥V, the notes paste) until 2026-09-17, when that moved to ⌘⌃V and left the board a pure character sheet. The word sheet renders through the same `KeymapOverlayRenderer` in `.word` style: the letter shrinks to the top-left corner to make room, the word wraps across the bottom at a size chosen by its length (`wordFontSize` — word-wrap cannot break a single word, so an oversized font spills over the key border instead of wrapping), and **keys with nothing bound are dimmed to 22% while bound ones keep the full-brightness border**, so the eye lands only on what's live. The ⌘⌃ image is built **in the controller's `init`, not from the layout file**: those are app shortcuts, not characters, so a failure to locate the `.keylayout` must not take the shortcut sheet down with it (which also means `images` is never empty — the layout retry now asks `images[.option] == nil`). Pressing any key under either modifier drops the overlay (`keyDownWhileModifierHeld`): the hold turned out to be a real shortcut. `CommandControlShortcuts.labels` is the map and **must stay in sync with the ⌘⌃ branches in `EventTapManager`**; a test asserts the exact key-code set. Two deliberate exceptions to that rule, both documented in the map: **⌘⌃W is on the sheet but NOT bound here** — it is Wispr Flow's "paste transcript", and the sheet answers "what does this combination do on this Mac", so an empty key would be the wrong answer. Since 2026-09 **neither ⌃W nor ⌘⌃W is bound in this app at all**: ⌃W belongs to `victor-effects` (the 🔥 whip), ⌘⌃W is Wispr Flow's, and the two coexist only because the effects tap's whip rule requires `!hasCmd`. The sheet's answer is unchanged — the key does something on this Mac, just not in this process; (**⌘⌃V used to be the second exception**, bound but deliberately blank, because while it meant the emotional paste it would have put a second "paste" on a board where ⌘⌃W already says it — and two keys reading the same word makes the reader pick wrong at speed. It read `spus` with a 🎙️ accent while it meant the transcript picker, and since 2026-09-17 it reads **`clipboard` with S's 🚀 accent**: the key is the clipboard→notes append now, moved off ⌃⌥V. The word had to be the *source* rather than the destination, because S one row over already says `notes` and two keys reading the same word is the very thing this key stayed blank for in its emotional-paste days; the repeated 🚀 is deliberate — the pair does the same thing to two different things, and a mark of its own would hide exactly that.) The **Emoji Overlay** menu item toggles all of them.
- **The ⌥ sheet is a palette, the ⌘⌃ sheet is a menu (`KeymapHoldCoordinator.keyDownWhileModifierHeld`, 2026-09-09)** — pressing a key while the modifiers were still held used to take *any* sheet down, on the theory that the hold had turned out to be a real shortcut rather than a question. That is right for ⌘⌃: the key just pressed **is** the choice, the shortcut has fired, and what stays on screen is a picture of a menu nobody is reading any more. It is exactly wrong for ⌥ / ⌥⇧, which is the case the sheet exists for — ⌥ is still down *because the next emoji is coming*, and hiding the layout after the first character hides it from the one person using it, someone picking a row of characters off a layout nobody has memorised. So only `.commandControl` tears down on a keypress now; the ⌥ layers stay up until ⌥ itself is released (`reset()`), and swapping ⇧ in and out mid-hold still switches between the two of them.
- **The ⌃⌥ board (2026-09-10)** — a third emoji layer, sitting beside ⌥ and ⌥⇧ in the same `EmojiKeyLayer` map file (`~/.victor-emoji-layer.json`, section `controlOption`) and drawn by the same renderer in `.glyph` style. It exists because **the ⌥ layer ran out of letters years ago**: 🪿 is on ⌥8 because G was long gone, and a digit is something you look up where a letter is something you remember. ⌃⌥ is also the least-colliding pair left on this Mac — macOS binds almost nothing to it, apps rarely do, where ⌥⌘ is the busiest combination there is. It opened with one key, **⌃⌥G = 🪿**, and ⌥8's goose stays exactly where it was: the new board adds a letter that means the word, it does not move a digit that already works. It grew two more the same afternoon — **⌃⌥B = 🐞** (🐛 until 2026-09-22 — same key, same meaning, but the caterpillar is not what "bug" pictures anywhere else; then the beetle 🪲 until 2026-10-02, when the ladybug replaced it as the nicer-looking bug) and **⌃⌥P = 🪂** — then **⌃⌥C = 😂** on 2026-09-12 and, on 2026-09-22, **⌃⌥A = 🚌**, **⌃⌥S = 🐑** (S was free here — the only nearby S is ⌘⌃S's note grab, on a different board) and **⌃⌥M = ✉️** (M was bound to nothing on this board, and ⌥M's 🤪 stays put), then **⌃⌥W = ⚔️** on 2026-10-03 (W for war: the Romanian *săbii* would want S, already the sheep's) (*autobuz*, the Romanian word: in English "bus" and "beetle" would both have wanted B), a letter that was free only because the selection-or-clipboard note grab had just left ⌃⌥C for ⌘⌃S — and the parachute is the case where a digit *did* move: it was on **⌥⇧8** and is now only here. The two are not a contradiction. ⌥8 is years of muscle memory and breaking it would cost more than the letter is worth; ⌥⇧8 was a digit on the shifted board that nobody had ever learned, so moving it broke nothing — and leaving 🪂 on both keys would only mean one of them gets typed by accident. The rule the board is settling into: a letter that means the word wins, unless the digit is already a habit. A seed change alone does **not** reach the running Mac — `~/.victor-emoji-layer.json` already exists, so the seed is only what a fresh machine starts from; both were edited, which is why the keys work without a rebuild. **No ⇧ variant, on purpose** (`EmojiKeyLayer.Layer`): ⌥ needed one because ⌥⇧ arrived as a full layout's worth of characters inherited from `Victor-v27`, while this board starts at one key — so ⌃⌥⇧G types the goose too, and asks for the same sheet, rather than reaching a second, empty one. A `.keylayout` could never have carried any of this: macOS derives control characters itself rather than asking the layout (which is why `Victor-v27`'s own ⌃ emoji sat inert for years), so the board only exists because the tap owns the rewrite — and for the same reason the rewrite must strip **⌃ as well as ⌥/⇧** off the event, or the character arrives as a chord and the app derives a control code from it instead of inserting it. The branch sits **above every ⌃⌥ shortcut in the tap**: a letter the map claims is an emoji key and nothing else, and one it doesn't claim falls straight through, so an unclaimed letter still falls through to whatever the tap binds (⌃⌥V appended the clipboard to the notes until 2026-09-17; it is unbound now). Adding a key is one line in the JSON and takes effect on the next keystroke — the sheet redraws itself off `EmojiKeyLayer.generation`. A map file written before this layer existed has its `controlOption` section **backfilled from the seed on load**, keeping every other section byte-for-byte: rewriting the file from the seeds instead would silently revert every emoji Victor has changed since.
- **😀 The ⌥⌥ emoji picker (`EmojiPickerController`, `EmojiBoard`, 2026-10-04)** — **left ⌥ tapped twice** (`OptionDoubleTap`: each press ≤ 0.35 s, releases ≤ 0.45 s apart, nothing else held or typed in between, right ⌥ breaks it — so ⌥-typing two emoji in a row never counts, and a hold stays the cheat-sheet's gesture) opens a picker for the emoji that are **not on a key** (and so does holding ⌃⇧ — see below). It was ⌃⌘Space for half an hour, Apple's own "Emoji & Symbols" chord; Victor asked for ⌥⌥ instead, so Apple's chord is untouched again. **Where:** the screen the ⌥ cheat-sheet picks (`KeymapOverlayPlacement`), **full screen** on the external, or a corner panel at the retina's bottom-right — 1× when the mouse is on the only external (0.45 of the retina's width since 2026-10-04 evening, 25% more than the first 0.36, at the same cell size), **1.3× on both axes when the retina is the only screen** (2026-10-04, everything scaled together; the rounded corner is decided by "is this a whole screen", no longer by `scale > 1`) — the board keeps its cell geometry in both, only scaled. **The board is a map, not a list** (Victor: *"niciodată nu voi da scroll prin toate, ci voi căuta sau apăsa pe unul pe care l-am reținut unde e poziție"*): 25 × 13 cells (20 × 10 until 2026-10-04 evening: Victor wanted the emoji a bit smaller on the external and the retina corner 25% bigger — both mean more cells; the saved board was laid out again **once** by `EmojiBoardStore.migrate`, each region replayed nearest-to-its-old-anchor first so every cluster keeps its shape, flagged by `EmojiPicker.boardLayout` = 2), **empty at first**; an emoji lands on it the first time it is picked and **never moves again** — reuse only bumps its timestamp, which is consulted solely to evict the least-recently-used one when all 325 cells are full (it takes that cell; nothing else shifts). A newcomer takes the free cell nearest its group's anchor: Smileys top-left, People top-middle, Animals top-right, Food middle of the left edge, **arrows bottom-left**, Travel + Activities bottom-middle, Objects + Symbols + Flags bottom-right — seven seeds, the board grows inward from them. The arrows (a Symbols name containing "arrow": ⬅️ ↩️ 🔄 🔝 — not 💘 / 🏹, other groups) got their own corner because Victor uses them a lot; Food moved up to make room. **The arrow corner is laid out by hand** (`EmojiBoard.arrowBlock`, 2026-10-04 — Victor: *"într-o ordine cu sens"*). Three roses in the bottom three rows with a free column between them — plain (cols 0–2), blue (4–6), hands 👆👈👉👇 (8–10, corners empty) — and above them two rows with each plain arrow right above its blue twin: ↩︎↪︎⤴︎⤵︎ ↺↻ 🔄🔃 ⏪⏩⏫⏬ 🔁🔂🔀 on top, ↩️↪️⤴️⤵️ ◀️▶️🔼🔽 ↔︎↕︎ ↔️↕️ under it. `""` in the block is a hole; holes (rose centres, gaps) are reserved too, so the shape never fills with stray emoji. All of them are put on the board at once; their cells are **reserved** (no other emoji lands there, none is evicted on a full board); a keyed one (↗ ⌥⇧U, 🔼 🔽 👉) leaves its cell empty so the order around the hole holds. The one-off migration moved the block's arrows to their cells keeping their "last used", and whatever sat on those cells to the free cell nearest its own anchor. Other arrows (🔙 ⤡ …) still grow around the block from the corner's anchor. **Plain arrows** ← ↑ → ↓ ↖︎ ↘︎ ↙︎ ↔︎ ↕︎ ↺ ↻ — no blue keycap — are not emoji and not in the generated catalogue, so `EmojiCatalog.plainArrows` adds them by hand (searchable: "plain", "simplă", "arrow"); those with an emoji twin carry FE0E so they stay distinct from it and land as text, and the board draws them in the system font (`EmojiPickerPolicy.isTextSymbol`), since Apple Color Emoji would draw ↔ as the blue tile even without FE0F. ↗ was left out while it was on ⌥⇧U (`keyedEmoji` also registers a keyed single character's FE0E twin); Victor gave ⌥⇧U up on 2026-10-04 evening (*"îl iau din ⌥⌥"*), so ↗︎ joined the plain arrows and layout version 5 dropped ↗︎ and ↗️ into their reserved rose cells — only those two, so arrows taken off by hand stay off. The migration put them and 🔄 on the board up front. The first version listed the whole catalogue by category under a "recently used" row; Victor rejected both the categories and the scrolling within minutes. **Nothing on a ⌥ / ⌥⇧ / ⌃⌥ key is ever on the board** (`EmojiPickerPolicy.keyedEmoji`, compared with FE0F and skin tones stripped): a keyed emoji is not recorded, and one that is put on a key later leaves the board on the next open, freeing its cell. **Search** (English *and* Romanian names + CLDR keywords, diacritic-insensitive: `piersica` finds 🍑; plus English **extra synonyms** in the catalogue's 7th column — emojilib's community tags (👍 "approve"), emojibase tags + GitHub/Slack/JoyPixels shortcodes, and emojidb.org's crowd votes, top 10 per word, cached in `tools/emojidb-keywords.json` by `tools/fetch-emojidb.py` (~5500 requests, resumable via `~/.cache/emojidb`), so "left" also finds 👈; **typo-tolerant** since 2026-10-04 — `EmojiFuzzy`, Damerau-Levenshtein (OSA) against the *prefix* of each vocabulary word, so a swap like "lfet" is one edit; none under 4 letters, 1 edit for 4-6, 2 from 7; ranking exact < prefix < fuzzy and name < keyword < extra synonym, so a typo never outranks a correct spelling) **puts its matches on the board itself** (since 2026-10-04 evening; Victor: *"matricea să se umple cu rezultate … dispuse în jurul centrului, iar selecția să fie pe cel din centru"*): best match in the centre cell, already selected, the next ones in rings around it (`EmojiBoard.cellsFromCentre` — by distance, clockwise from the right within a ring, so ranks 2–5 are one arrow away), up to all 325 cells; emptying the field brings the saved board back, on the last one used. It replaced a one-row **strip beside the field** (~8 cells on the retina corner), which was meant to keep the board still while typing but cut ½ off for "half". **What you picked for what you typed comes first** (`EmojiQueryMemory`, 2026-10-04 — Victor: *"să rețină ce am ales când am scris ce texte … mic > 🎙️"*): a pick made from a search is stored under its folded query (`UserDefaults` `EmojiPicker.queryPicks`, count + last date, at most 500 queries, the stalest forgotten first); the same query later puts it in the centre, and a query still on its way to a remembered one (≥ 2 letters: "mi" → "mic") lifts it right after the exact ones — most picked, then most recent. It only reorders what the search found anyway, so the explanation stays true, and the name line adds, in amber, *ales de 3× pentru „mic”*. Otherwise order is by exactness: score first, then the **shorter name** (the query is more of what the emoji is called — "jumătate" is all of ½'s Romanian name but a fifth of 🕧's), then Unicode order. Keyed emoji do show among the matches, with their chord in amber under them — searching for one you forgot is on a key is when the chord is worth seeing; no ✕ appears while searching. **Why it matched** (Victor: *"de ce tastând aff apărea 💒?"* — the crowd synonym "affection"): while searching, the name line starts, right under the box, with the vocabulary word each typed word was found in, its matched letters in bold amber (`EmojiFuzzy.matchedLetters`: the prefix for an exact/prefix match, else the equal letters of the cheapest OSA alignment walked back, so a typo's wrong letter stays white) and its source — *(nume)* or *(cuvânt-cheie)* after it, or a `~` in front of a crowd synonym (`~affection`); a Romanian word is shown with its diacritics and a 🇷🇴 after it (`EmojiEntry.romanian`: the words of the Romanian name and keywords that the English ones lack — a word both share, like "text", gets no flag) (`EmojiCatalog.explain`, same costs as `search`). **Typographic symbols** that are not emoji — ½ ⅓ ⅔ ¼ ¾ ° × ÷ ± ≈ ≠ ≤ ≥ ∞ ∑ µ … ⋯ · • « » – — ✓ ↷ ⓶, chosen from what Apple's picker remembers him using — are added by hand in `EmojiCatalog.textSymbols` (en + ro names, synonyms such as "1/2", "jumătate", "fracție") and drawn in the system font. ↵ picks the selection, ←→↑↓ move (to the nearest placed emoji in that direction — cell by cell among the matches), Esc closes, a click outside closes it. **Look:** victor-effects' soundboard panel — near-black plate (opaque here: a bright slide ghosted through 0.94), grey tiles with 6 pt corners, darker empty cells, #39FF14 green ring on hover, #FF2121 red on press with the pick on release, 0.20 s slide-in from the right. **Focus: it never takes it** (since 2026-10-04 afternoon). Victor drops emoji into **ScreenBrush's text mode** (⌘ + its key, drag a text box, then ⌥-emoji), and ScreenBrush leaves text mode the moment it stops being the active app — which the picker did on every open while it activated itself to give its search box the keyboard. Now the panel can never become key and the app is never activated: while it is up, `EventTapManager` swallows each keystroke and hands it over decoded (`EmojiPickerKey`: text, ↵, ⌫, arrows, Esc; a ⌘ / ⌃ chord passes through and closes it), the search box is a label with a drawn caret, a click on the non-activating panel moves no focus, and a click anywhere else closes it (global mouse monitor). The pick is typed into whatever still has focus. History of the focus question: a key-taking non-activating panel lost the keyboard to PowerPoint in 0.3 s → activating fixed that and broke ScreenBrush → tap-fed input needs neither. **Second way in: ⌃⇧ held** (`EmojiPickerHold`, Victor: *"poate să apară și la ctrl+shift ținute apăsat? … voi face click, nu voi tasta"*) — the soundboard's hold-and-click: after the cheat-sheet's hold delay (0.3 s / 1 s single screen) the board appears, a click picks, letting go closes it; a key pressed under ⌃⇧ means the hold was a shortcut and cancels it until the next fresh hold; a picker opened by ⌥ ×2 is not closed by a ⌃⇧ release. Because a keystroke posted under the hold reaches the app merged with the held ⌃⇧ (a shortcut, not text), a pick made with modifiers down is **written straight into the focused text box through Accessibility** (`FocusedTextInsertion`: set `kAXSelectedText`, verified by the value actually changing) so it is there on the click — Victor: *"să nu fie nevoie să ridic Control Shift ca să văd deja textul"*; the first version waited for the release. Only if the element refuses AX does it fall back to **typing the moment no modifier is physically down** (polled every 20 ms, up to 5 s); the log says which path ran. With no modifier held (⌥ ×2 + ↵ or click) the keystroke is used, since it works in every app and AX does not. **Catalogue:** `Resources/emoji-catalog.json`, generated by `tools/build-emoji-catalog.py` from Unicode's `emoji-test.txt` (order, groups), macOS CoreEmoji `AppleName.strings` en + ro (names, and the filter for "this Mac's font can draw it" — 1904 emoji on macOS 15) and CLDR annotations en + ro (keywords); rerun it after a macOS upgrade that adds emoji. The board lives in `UserDefaults` (`EmojiPicker.board`). **Seeded once from Apple's picker** (`MacEmojiHistory`, `EmojiBoard.importHistory`, Victor: *"pune deja pe cele recent folosite … și altele mai vechi"*): `com.apple.EmojiPreferences` → `EMFUsageHistoryKey` maps each emoji to the sequence numbers of its uses (245 emoji on 2026-10-04), so the import ranks by **use count, then recency** and places the most used first — they get the cells nearest their anchors. It skips emoji already on the board (they stay put), keyed ones, Apple's non-emoji symbols (← ° µ), counts a skin-toned use for its base, stops at 140 cells (`importCap`) so the board can still grow, and gives imports a 1970 "last used" so a full board evicts them before anything picked here. **Removing one by hand:** rest the mouse 0.8 s on a tile and a red ✕ disc appears on its top-right corner (half outside the tile, so it hides little of the emoji; moving onto it does not count as leaving the tile); clicking it takes the emoji off the board (`EmojiBoard.remove`) — its cell is free for the next newcomer, nothing else moves, and picking it again later simply lands it afresh (Victor: *"dacă las mai mult mouse-ul pe o dală … un X roșu în colțul dălei"*). The delay is so a sweep across the board never flashes a row of ✕s. The `EmojiPicker.importedMacHistory` flag makes it one-shot: re-importing later would drop newcomers in among cells Victor has already learnt.
